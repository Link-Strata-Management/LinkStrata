Your Link to Smarter Strata Management

Thinking of Changing Strata Managers?

Let LINK help you move to a more proactive, transparent, and dependable strata management experience.

The right strata manager should make life easier, not harder.

Changing strata managers is not a decision that Owners Corporations make lightly.

In most cases, the decision comes after ongoing frustrations with communication, administration, maintenance coordination, or a lack of confidence that the community's needs are being met.

At LINK Strata Management, we regularly speak with Executive Committees that have reached a point where they simply want better support, clearer communication, and practical advice.

About

LINK Strata Management (a division of LINK Corporate Services Pty Ltd, established in 1998) delivers expert administration and management solutions tailored to the needs of residential and commercial property communities. Built on a foundation of accounting excellence, LINK offers prompt, efficient service backed by a team of highly skilled professionals.
We understand the increasing complexity and time pressures faced by Executive Committees, and we’re committed to providing practical, strategic support that addresses the concerns of both owners and committee members. Our goal is to simplify strata management by offering reliable access to administration, maintenance, compliance, and financial resources — all within a cost-effective framework.
At LINK, we take the frustration out of property management, so committee members can focus on enjoying the benefits of ownership while we handle the rest.

Our Services

Professional Financial Management

LINK’s financial management practices promote full transparency and empowers owners with a clear understanding their Unit Title's financial pposition. With our expert oversight and reliable reporting, you can rest easy knowing your property is in capable hands.

Insurance

LINK ensures your property remains fully insured in accordance with current legislation. We work closely with your chosen insurer or trusted insurance broker to manage claims, valuations, quotations, and renewals—while collaborating with owners to optimise coverage and control costs.

Maintenance & Repairs

At LINK, we understand that prompt attention to maintenance is essential to preserving the value of your property. That’s why we treat every issue with urgency and care. We prioritise clear communication and transparency throughout the process—arranging quotes, coordinating repairs, and ensuring all work is completed to a professional standard and within budget.

Administration Management

At LINK, we understand that accurate record-keeping is the foundation of effective strata management. Our processes are built for precision and detail, and we work hard to ensure our team stays up to date with legislative changes and industry developments—so we can support our Units Plans with timely, informed service.

Tailored Porfolio Management

At LINK, we recognise that every client has unique needs and evolving expectations. Our dedicated team takes a proactive approach to developing tailored solutions that meet your specific requirements with precision and care.

Governance

LINK’s dedicated team ensures your Units Plan remains compliant with the Unit Titles (Management) Act 2011 We support Executive Committees by coordinating with solicitors, assisting in the drafting, reviewing, and amending of rules, managing crown lease renewals, and issuing formal notices to owners and tenants for rule breaches. Our proactive approach helps streamline governance and reduce administrative burden.

The LINK Approach

Effective Communication

Effective communication is the foundation of understanding, collaboration, and trust. It enables us to resolve differences, foster mutual respect, and create an environment where innovative ideas and practical solutions can thrive.

Dedication

LINK’s approach centres on dedicated staff who respond directly to your unique requirements. By maintaining a lower-than-industry-average portfolio per Strata Manager, we ensure more focused administration and deliver a higher standard of service to every property we manage.

Flexibility

At LINK, we follow well-defined processes and proven methods to deliver reliable and efficient strata management services. We understand that every Units Plan is unique, and we welcome the opportunity to tailor our approach to meet your specific needs.
