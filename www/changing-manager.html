Changing Strata Managers

Every community is different, but some concerns arise time and time again.

Common Reasons Owners Corporations Consider a New Strata Manager

Communication Has Become a Challenge

Committee members shouldn't have to follow up repeatedly for responses or updates.

You may be experiencing:

  • Delayed responses to emails and phone calls
  • Limited updates on ongoing matters
  • Difficulty obtaining advice when decisions need to be made
  • Frustration from owners due to a lack of communication

Financial Information Isn't Clear

Strong financial management is one of the most important responsibilities of a strata manager.

Warning signs can include:

  • Difficulty understanding financial reports
  • Limited guidance during budget preparation
  • Concerns regarding levy arrears
  • Unexpected expenses or special levies
  • Little visibility over long-term financial planning

Maintenance Feels Reactive

A well-managed Owners Corporation should be planning for future maintenance, not simply responding to issues as they arise.

Common concerns include:

  • Delays in addressing maintenance requests
  • Difficulty managing contractors
  • Lack of preventative maintenance planning
  • Uncertainty around future capital works

The Committee Is Taking On Too Much

Committee members volunteer their time to support their community.

When committees find themselves:

  • Chasing contractors
  • Managing administrative tasks
  • Following up outstanding matters
  • Interpreting legislation
  • Coordinating repairs and projects

It may be a sign that additional support is needed.

What Should You Expect From Your Strata Manager?

A professional strata management partnership should provide:

  • Clear and timely communication
  • Financial transparency
  • Practical advice and guidance
  • Proactive maintenance management
  • Support with governance and compliance
  • Confidence that matters are being managed appropriately

Your strata manager should be helping the Committee make informed decisions while reducing its administrative burden.

Changing Strata Managers Is Often Easier Than You Think

One of the most common concerns we hear is: "It sounds like too much work to change."

In reality, the process is often straightforward when properly managed.

LINK can assist your Owners Corporation through the transition process, including:

  1. Initial discussion regarding your community's requirements.
  2. Review of current management arrangements.
  3. Appointment of LINK Strata Management.
  4. Transfer of records, financial information, contracts and documentation.
  5. Introduction to your dedicated management team and commencement of services.

Our goal is to ensure a smooth transition with minimal disruption to owners and residents.
